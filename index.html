Privacy Policy

Rankle is an Elo ranking app for pickleball groups. This page explains exactly what we store, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

Ads & trackingNone. No advertising or analytics SDKs, no data sold.
Precise locationNever requested. City and country on a public profile are typed by you.
DeletionIn the app, in Settings → Privacy & account. Immediate.

Who we are

Rankle is operated by an independent developer. For any privacy request, email zaryababutt@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

What we collect

  • Account — if you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your email address, a stable account identifier, and (if you grant it) your name. Apple may give us a private relay email. We never receive your password.
  • Profile — the display name you choose, an optional avatar photo, and — only if you publish a global profile — a city and two-letter country you type yourself.
  • Gameplay — groups you belong to, roster names, match results (players, score, date), paddle tags, and the group activity log. Ratings are calculated from these results.
  • Purchases — whether Rankle Pro is active, its expiry date, and the store product identifier. Payment details are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card.
  • Safety — reports you file (reason, optional details, the reported player), players you block, and moderation outcomes.
  • Technical — short-lived rate-limit counters and standard server logs (timestamp, request path) kept to stop abuse and keep the service up.

What we do not collect: precise or background location, contacts, calendar, health data, SMS, call logs, advertising identifiers, biometrics, or your device's photo library beyond the single image you pick as an avatar. The app does not request camera or microphone access.

Why we use it

  • To run the leaderboard, match history and rankings you asked for (contract).
  • To authenticate you and keep your groups separate (legitimate interest / contract).
  • To grant Pro features and validate purchases (contract).
  • To review reports, block abusive players and prevent cheating, spam and invite-code guessing (legitimate interest, and legal obligations for illegal content).
  • To meet tax, accounting and consumer-protection obligations (legal obligation).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling with legal effect, or automated decisions that significantly affect you.

Who can see it

  • Your groups can see your roster name, avatar, matches and the activity log. That is the product.
  • Other Rankle players can see your display name, public ID, city, country, Elo and games only if you turn on a public global profile. It is off by default, even for Pro users.
  • Nobody else. Global rankings exclude anyone you have blocked.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase (our processor) and reached only over HTTPS with per-user authentication. Row-level security and server-side functions enforce that a member can only touch their own groups. A local cache lives on your device so the app works offline; group keys and your session are kept in the iOS Keychain / Android Keystore, not in plain files.

Processors we share with

  • Supabase — database, authentication, edge functions (hosting region: as configured in the Supabase project).
  • RevenueCat — subscription state, so Pro follows your account across devices. We send a pseudonymous user ID and receipt information, never your card.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — payment processing and subscription management.
  • Google / Apple sign-in — identity verification, if you choose that button.
  • Expo / EAS — building and distributing the app binary.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. International transfers rely on the processors' standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile — until you delete your account, then removed immediately.
  • Match history — until a group member or the commissioner deletes it, or the group is deleted. When you delete your account, matches stay on the group's record with you listed as “Former player”, so other players' ratings stay reproducible.
  • Avatars — until you change or remove the photo, or delete your account.
  • Reports and moderation records — up to 12 months after resolution.
  • Rate-limit counters — 7 days. Activity log — up to 2 years.

Your rights

  • Access & portability — Settings → Privacy & account → “Export my account data” produces a JSON file of your account, memberships, matches you logged, blocks and reports.
  • Deletion — Settings → Privacy & account → “Delete my account”. This deletes your account, entitlement, public profile and avatar, and unlinks you from every group. No email required.
  • Correction — edit your display name, avatar and profile in the app.
  • Objection / restriction — email us; we will stop processing unless we have a compelling legal basis.
  • Complaint — you may complain to your local data-protection authority (GDPR) or the FTC/state attorney general (US).

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we honour deletion and access requests through the in-app controls above. Canadian residents: you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

All traffic uses TLS. Sessions and group keys are stored in the platform keychain. The Android build disables cloud backup of app data, release builds are minified, and server functions verify identity on every call. No system is perfect: if you find a vulnerability, please email us before disclosing it publicly.

Children

Rankle is for players aged 13 and over. We ask every account to confirm their age group and we block under-13 sign-ups. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it. Users aged 13–17 should have a parent or guardian review this policy with them. We do not show ads, so no personal data is used for advertising to minors.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we update the effective date and ask you to accept the new Terms in the app before continuing. The current version is stored with your account.
