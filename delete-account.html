Delete your account

You can delete your Rankle account and personal data at any time, without emailing us.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rankle and go to Settings.
  2. Under Privacy & account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm the prompt. Deletion happens immediately.

If you cannot open the app, use the email option below and we will delete the account for you.

What is deleted

  • Your Rankle account, sign-in identity and Pro entitlement.
  • Your public profile, avatar, city and country.
  • Your group memberships and any blocks or reports you filed.
  • Your device's local copy of the group data, on that device.

What stays

Matches you logged stay on the group's record with you listed as “Former player”. Ratings are recomputed from match history, so removing those matches would silently change everyone else's rating. The remaining data is no longer linked to your account.

Request deletion by email

Email zaryababutt@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Rankle account deletion”. We action these within 7 days and reply when it is done. If you are not sure which address you used, include your group name and public ID so we can find the account.

Want a copy first?

In the app, Settings → Privacy & account → Export my account data downloads a JSON file with your account, memberships and the matches you logged.
